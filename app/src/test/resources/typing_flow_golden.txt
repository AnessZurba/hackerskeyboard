# What typing leaves in the field (TypingFlowTest); | is the cursor.
# Regenerate with -PupdateGolden.

## sentence field
"hello world"        -> "Hello world|"
"hi. how are you"    -> "Hi. How are you|"
"hi  there"          -> "Hi  there|"
"hi, there! ok? yes" -> "Hi, there! Ok? Yes|"
"dr.who"             -> "Dr.who|"
"it's 5 o'clock"     -> "It's 5 o'clock|"
"hello\b\b\b"        -> "He|"
"hi \b\bey"          -> "Hey|"
"word\nnext"         -> "Wordnext|" (editorAction 6)
"⇧a⇧b c"             -> "aB c|"
"a , b"              -> "A , b|"
"end. "              -> "End. |" shifted
"see you ."          -> "See you .|"
