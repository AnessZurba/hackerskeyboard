Word suggestion dictionaries (AOSP binary dictionary format v2)
=================================================================

These files are data, not code, and are distributed under their own licences, which
differ from the app's (Apache License 2.0). They are built with AOSP's own dicttool
(tools/aosp-dicttool/makedict.sh) from the sources below; tools/mkcombined.py prepares
the word lists.

main_en.dict - English
    Source: AOSP LatinIME, dictionaries/en_US_wordlist.combined.gz, commit 127336e9
    https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/inputmethods/LatinIME
    Licence: Apache License 2.0. Copyright (C) The Android Open Source Project.
    Changes: none (converted as is).

main_iw.dict - Hebrew
    Source: AOSP LatinIME, dictionaries/iw_wordlist.combined.gz, commit 127336e9
    (same repository). Licence: Apache License 2.0. Copyright (C) The Android Open
    Source Project.
    Changes: 94,728 of 94,799 words kept: words with Latin letters or digits, and five
    run-together words with a final letter inside, were dropped. The list has no niqqud.

main_ar.dict - Arabic
    Source: Leipzig Corpora Collection (Wortschatz, Leipzig University),
    https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download , corpora ara_news_2020_1M and
    ara_wikipedia_2021_1M (word frequency lists, "*-words.txt").
    Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Citation: D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart, U. Quasthoff: Building Large Monolingual
    Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages.
    In: Proceedings of LREC 2012.
    Changes: the counts of both corpora were added up after removing harakat, the
    superscript alef and tatweel (NFKC); only single words made of the 36 letters of the
    Arabic alphabet (U+0621-U+063A, U+0641-U+064A) were kept; the 150,000 most frequent
    were turned into dictionary frequencies (210 + 21 * log10(count / top count)).
    Licence of this dictionary: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
    Leipzig publishes its word lists under CC BY 4.0, but one of the two counts comes
    from Wikipedia text, which is CC BY-SA 4.0; to be safe this file is distributed under
    the share-alike licence. That applies to this data file only, not to the app's code.

The word lists for adaptive key targets (assets/wordlists) carry their sources in their
headers: en.txt and he.txt from the same AOSP lists (Apache License 2.0); ar.txt from
Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018 (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), distributed under the same licence.

Dictionaries for other languages are not in the app: they are optional downloads, each
with its source and licence on the app's Dictionaries screen. AOSP LatinIME word lists
(Apache License 2.0) for cs da de el es fi fr hr it lt lv nb nl pl pt_PT ro ru sl sr sv tr,
as published in HeliBoard's dictionary repository (codeberg.org/Helium314/aosp-dictionaries,
a pinned commit); the AOSP pt_BR list and Leipzig Corpora Collection news corpora (CC BY
4.0) for uk bg hu sk ca, made by tools/mkdicts.sh and published on this repository's
"dicts-v1" release with a NOTICE.txt.
